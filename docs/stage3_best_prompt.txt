You are classifying a customer support ticket.

Pick exactly one queue, one type, one priority, and one or more tags. Use only the values listed below, spelled exactly as written. Do not invent values. Do not default to the same queue, type, priority, or tags; base each choice on the ticket text.

Queue descriptions:
- Billing and Payments: Billing issues and payment processing. Use for invoices, charges, refunds, subscriptions, payment methods, pricing inquiries, and financial account matters.
- Customer Service: Customer inquiries and service requests. Use for existing-customer how-to questions, explanations, benefits, and general assistance that is not primarily sales, billing, IT, or product troubleshooting.
- General Inquiry: General inquiries and information requests. Use for non-account-specific questions, feedback, feature/enhancement requests not tied to a specific product, and general information requests not tied to a specific product failure. Also use for security incidents that are not tied to a specific product or internal system and are not primarily requests for sales/pre-sales guidance.
- Human Resources: Employee inquiries and HR-related issues. Use only for internal employee or HR matters. Also use for internal requests for training, performance improvement, or team development.
- IT Support: Internal IT support and infrastructure issues. Use for internal systems, developer tools, software installation/update/upgrade, compatibility fixes, access, and infrastructure.
- Product Support: Support for product-related issues. Use for problems with a specific product's functionality, usage, configuration, or bugs when it is the customer's product, not internal IT. Also use for feature requests and update requests for a specific product, and for product/service problems involving marketing, brand growth, or business strategy when tied to a specific product/service.
- Returns and Exchanges: Product returns and exchanges. Use for returns, replacements, exchanges, and related logistics. Also use for integration or data-exchange inquiries when the ticket asks for detailed technical options, limitations, restrictions, or data types involved in exchanging data between products.
- Sales and Pre-Sales: Sales inquiries and pre-sales questions. Use for explicit purchasing, quotes, demos, trials, licensing, or product-fit questions before purchase. Also use for security-related requests that primarily ask for guidance, recommendations, additional measures, or solutions to secure a system, when not tied to a specific product failure or internal IT system.
- Service Outages and Maintenance: Service interruptions and maintenance. Use for outages, downtime, degraded service, planned maintenance windows, and requests to implement security updates or restore functionality when recovery, restoration, or maintenance is involved. Use primarily for the company's own services or internal service operations.
- Technical Support: Technical issues and support requests. Use for technical troubleshooting, errors, configuration problems, and technical how-to questions for existing users. Use for customer-reported technical issues involving multiple tools or software when no single product is the primary focus.

Type descriptions:
- Change: Planned change or update. Use for requests to update, upgrade, modify, configure, or enhance a system/product, including feature requests and planned improvements, and planned security updates/patches.
- Incident: Unexpected issue requiring immediate attention. Use for outages, security breaches, data loss, major malfunctions, and unplanned service interruptions. Note: Incident does not automatically imply high priority.
- Problem: Underlying issue causing multiple incidents. Use when the ticket describes a recurring root cause affecting multiple cases, including recurring crashes, errors, underperformance, or performance problems affecting multiple users.
- Request: Routine inquiry or service request. Use for pure information requests, how-to questions, account/service requests, and non-change/non-incident/non-problem tasks.

Priority descriptions:
- low: Non-urgent. Use for general inquiries, routine updates, simple feature requests, feedback, documentation questions, minor inconveniences, sporadic login failures that do not block work, ongoing underperformance that is not blocking work, and incidents that are not blocking work or are already being handled.
- medium: Moderately urgent. Use for performance issues, intermittent errors, compatibility problems that impede workflow, integration limitations, unauthorized access attempts that are not confirmed breaches, update/upgrade requests with compatibility issues, and detailed user questions needing timely resolution.
- high: Urgent. Use only for system outages, security breaches, data loss, major malfunctions, or when work is completely blocked and immediate attention is required. Do not use high for ordinary information requests or for incidents that are not causing complete work stoppage. Use high for security configuration requests involving sensitive data, medical data, or critical systems when immediate protection is needed.

Tags:
- Account & Access: Login, authentication, authorization, permissions, account management, and access issues.
- Billing & Returns: Invoices, charges, refunds, subscriptions, payment methods, pricing inquiries, billing options, and return/exchange-related billing matters.
- Bugs & Errors: Bugs, crashes, errors, malfunctions, or unexpected product behavior.
- Business Strategy: Business strategy, growth strategy, operational strategy, or business performance.
- Documentation & Training: Requests for instructions, documentation, step-by-step guides, training, explanations, or benefits.
- Feedback & Feature Requests: Requests for enhancements, improvements, new capabilities, updates, changes, or feedback. Also use for inquiries about potential capabilities, future offerings, or improvements that could inform a feature or service request.
- General Support: General assistance, how-to, service requests, or routine support not covered by a more specific tag.
- Hardware & Infrastructure: Hardware, servers, networks, infrastructure components, system architecture, devices, and physical or virtual infrastructure.
- Integration: Connecting, syncing, exchanging data, or integrating two or more systems/products. Only use when the primary issue or request is about integration, not just a mention.
- Marketing & Brand: Marketing campaigns, brand growth, audience targeting, content strategy, or brand perception.
- Outages & Recovery: Service outages, downtime, degraded service, planned maintenance, and recovery from incidents (including security breaches or data loss).
- Performance: Speed, reliability, scalability, workflow efficiency, or compatibility that affects performance. Only use when performance is the primary issue, not just mentioned as a benefit.
- Sales: Pricing, purchasing, quotes, demos, licensing, or sales-related questions. Also use for explicit requests about sales strategy, marketing strategy, or brand growth initiatives, and for inquiries about services offered or sales-related solution fit.
- Security: Encryption, access control, audits, confidentiality, integrity, availability, data protection, or breaches.

Important disambiguation rules:
- For login/access issues: Include Account & Access. Include Security if authentication or access control is involved. Queue Technical Support or Product Support if it is about a customer's specific product or technical troubleshooting for existing users. Queue IT Support if it is an internal system, developer tool, or infrastructure access issue. Queue Human Resources only for internal employee account or HR-system access matters. Priority low for sporadic or minor login failures that do not block work; medium if it impedes workflow; high only if complete work stoppage or security breach.
- For security-related tickets:
  * If the ticket reports an active breach, data loss, unauthorized access, or immediate security risk tied to a specific product or internal system, queue Technical Support or Product Support (or IT Support for internal systems). Type Incident. Priority high. Include Security. Include Outages & Recovery if recovery is needed. Include General Support if assistance is requested. Include Hardware & Infrastructure if infrastructure is involved.
  * If the ticket reports a security incident (e.g., unauthorized access attempts) not confirmed as an active breach, queue Product Support if tied to the customer's product/service, or Technical Support for general technical security issues. Type Incident. Priority medium unless there is confirmed breach/data loss/immediate risk, then high. Include Security. Include General Support. Include Hardware & Infrastructure if the ticket concerns systems, networks, or infrastructure. Do not include Outages & Recovery unless service is down or recovery is needed.
  * If the ticket primarily asks for guidance, configuration instructions, recommendations, or solutions to secure a system, and it is not tied to a specific product failure or internal IT system, queue Technical Support for existing-system technical configuration/guidance. Type Request. Priority high if sensitive data, medical data, or critical systems are involved and immediate protection is needed; otherwise medium or low. Include Security, General Support, and Documentation & Training. Do not include Sales unless explicitly about purchasing, licensing, or pre-sales.
  * If the ticket asks to implement security updates, patches, or restoration of functionality after security issues, queue Service Outages and Maintenance if it involves recovery, restoration, or maintenance. Type Change if planned; Incident if unplanned. Priority high if security/data protection and restoration of functionality are involved; otherwise medium or low. Include Security and General Support. Include Feedback & Feature Requests only if explicitly requesting new capabilities.
- For billing or pricing questions: queue Billing and Payments, not Sales and Pre-Sales, unless explicitly a pre-sales purchase question. Include Billing & Returns. Include Sales only if actually about selling, purchasing, quotes, demos, or licensing.
- For software update or upgrade requests: If it concerns internal tools, developer tools, or infrastructure, queue IT Support. If it concerns a customer's product, queue Product Support. Type Change. Priority medium if compatibility or workflow issues are mentioned; otherwise low. Include Feedback & Feature Requests and General Support when appropriate.
- For integration inquiries: Include Integration only if the ticket is primarily about connecting, syncing, or exchanging data. For detailed technical integration options, limitations, restrictions, or data types, queue Returns and Exchanges and use priority medium. For integration errors or troubleshooting, queue Product Support or Technical Support. Use type Request unless the ticket asks to implement or change an integration, in which case use Change.
- For product bugs, crashes, errors, or malfunctions: Queue Product Support if tied to a specific product the customer uses; Technical Support if it is a technical troubleshooting request for existing users; Service Outages and Maintenance if it is an outage, downtime, or degraded service of the company's own service. Type Problem if the ticket describes a recurring root cause affecting multiple cases or multiple users; Incident if it is a single unexpected issue requiring immediate attention; Change if it is a planned update/fix. Priority high for major malfunctions, data loss, security breaches, or complete work stoppage; medium for intermittent errors, performance issues, or workflow impediments; low for minor inconveniences. Include Bugs & Errors. Include Performance if speed, reliability, scalability, or compatibility is the primary issue. Include Outages & Recovery if service is degraded, down, crashing, or recovery is needed. Include General Support if assistance is requested.
- For multiple tools or products ceasing to function or causing disruption: If the ticket is a technical troubleshooting request for existing users involving multiple tools/software (e.g., digital marketing tools), queue Technical Support. Type Incident if it is an unplanned service interruption. Priority high if it causes major disruption or complete work stoppage. Include Outages & Recovery, Hardware & Infrastructure, Performance, and General Support. Do not include Bugs & Errors unless a specific bug/error is the primary issue. Do not queue Product Support unless a specific product is the primary focus. Do not use type Problem unless a recurring root cause affecting multiple cases/users is described.
- For marketing, brand growth, or business strategy problems: If tied to a specific product or service the customer uses, queue Product Support. If general and not tied to a specific product, queue General Inquiry. If it is an existing-customer how-to or explanation, queue Customer Service. Type Problem if it describes a recurring root cause or ongoing underperformance; Request if pure information; Change if requesting a change/improvement. Priority low unless it blocks work or is urgent. Include Marketing & Brand and/or Business Strategy. Include Performance if performance/efficiency is the primary issue. Include General Support if assistance is requested. Do not use Sales and Pre-Sales unless the ticket is explicitly about purchasing, quotes, demos, trials, licensing, or product-fit.
- For detailed information requests about services, tools, techniques, data types, methods, or recommendations: Queue General Inquiry if it is not account-specific and not tied to a specific product failure. Queue Customer Service for existing-customer how-to, explanations, benefits, or general assistance. Queue Sales and Pre-Sales if the request is explicitly about purchasing, quotes, demos, trials, licensing, or product-fit. Type Request. Priority medium for detailed user questions needing timely resolution; low for simple general information. Include Documentation & Training. Include Sales if asking about services offered or sales-related questions. Include Feedback & Feature Requests if asking about potential capabilities, future offerings, or improvements. Include General Support if routine assistance is requested.
- For service disruptions: queue Service Outages and Maintenance if it is an outage, downtime, or degraded service of the company's own service or planned maintenance. Type Incident. Priority low if work is only hampered and not completely blocked; medium if it impedes workflow; high if it is a major outage causing complete work stoppage. Include Outages & Recovery. Include General Support if assistance is requested. For customer-reported outages of multiple third-party tools or software, use Technical Support as described above.
- For internal employee inquiries, HR matters, training, performance improvement, or team development: queue Human Resources. Type Incident if it is an unexpected performance issue requiring attention, otherwise Request or Change. Priority low unless urgent. Include Performance, Documentation & Training, General Support, and Feedback & Feature Requests as supported.
- For enhancement, improvement, or feature requests: If it is for a specific product the customer uses, queue Product Support. If it is a general request not tied to a specific product, queue General Inquiry. Type Change. Priority low unless urgent or causing workflow blockage (then medium/high). Include Feedback & Feature Requests. Include Documentation & Training if the request involves learning about or using new features. Include General Support if assistance is needed.

Do not use tags that are not in the list above. Do not choose Sales and Pre-Sales, Request, or low by default. Justify each choice from the ticket text.
